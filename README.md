# SAME SIDE

Independent artist strategy portfolio by YOOYOUNG.

LIZ x REI original unit IP proposal.

A buildless static site: 12 scenes and a full production brief in English, covering the artist strategy, music direction, wardrobe direction and pilot criteria. Scene 03 presents full-length portraits with a separate heading and captions. Seven artist photographs and four garment-only Maison de VYNN photographs appear once each on the main page, and are bundled locally; image-sources.json records original image URLs and checksums. The wardrobe section uses cropped garment-only photographs and fabric details from two coordinated black-and-silver designs, with source links. Performer photographs, school-themed collections and design drawings are excluded from the wardrobe section. No generated fashion illustrations are included. Main music sections present a vocal-led UK garage direction, a proposed lyrical scene and two official-audio links: All I Want (Sunship Radio Edit) as the primary reference and Flowers (Sunship Edit) as support. Both are Sunship productions. Detailed commissioning and guide-session plans live in briefs.html. The 130–134 BPM range is a proposed demo target, not a measured reference tempo. Final key, tempo and vocal allocation require guide vocals. No synthesized player is included.

Sources reviewed 14 October 2026. The proposed pilot respects the announced group EP Looks Can Kill and requires a separately approved rehearsal window.

This is a proposed project, not an announced artist release or an approved brand collaboration.
