07 / Song concept

Two points
of view.

A night out, told from both sides: one wants to stay, the other is ready to leave. Their back-and-forth becomes a chorus about choosing the same way home.

Songwriter and producer brief ↗
01 / First verse

Set the scene.

Start with one person's reason for staying. Use specific, conversational lines that sound like they are addressed to someone beside her.

02 / Second viewpoint

Let the other person answer.

Give the other voice a different reason to leave. Change the phrasing and perspective while keeping the same groove.

03 / Shared chorus

Choose the same way home.

Bring both voices together on the title line. The chorus resolves the conversation with a choice they make together.
