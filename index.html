01 / Harmonic lift

Change the light.
Keep the tonal family.

Aadd9 → E/G# → F#m9 → Dmaj7

F# minor and A major share a key signature. Opening the hook on A adds relative-major color; these chords alone do not establish a modulation. Raise the top voicing and hold the bass longer to make the lift audible.

02 / Vocal arrangement

REI

Close, dry phrasing. Leave gaps after consonants; answer with short bass notes. Keep whispered tags behind the lead.

LIZ

Longer melodic line. Widen doubles at the pre-chorus; keep the lead centered. Test the upper harmony before assigning it.

Arrangement proposals, not claims about either member’s vocal range.

The decision in the room

Three clean hook takes while moving. If the line needs strain or an octave escape, revise the key or melody. If the hook loses its words, clear the pluck and vocal chops first.
